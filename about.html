Letters Journey

A website that teaches children to read the Holy Quran step by step: from the shape and sound of each Arabic letter, to vowel marks, to the rules of recitation. Through play and sound, with no reading skills needed to start.

The learning path: 15 lessons

Following the order of the “Al-Qa'idah Al-Dhahabiyyah” (Golden Primer) for Quran recitation. Each lesson opens after passing the one before it.

How each lesson works

📖 Short stages→🎮 Two games→🏆 A test→🏅 A named certificate

In each stage the child sees the letter large, hears its correct pronunciation and repeats it, then practises. Passing the test earns a certificate with the child's name, grade and their teacher's stamp.
